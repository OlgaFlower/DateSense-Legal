# User Privacy Choices

_Last updated: 2026-08-25_

SeeSi provides in-app controls that let you manage your data, what is shared, and who can open the app.

## In-app privacy controls

Open **Settings** in the app to find these options.

---

## Share anonymous statistics

**Menu path:** Settings → Privacy → **Share anonymous statistics**

**What this does**
- Turns app analytics on or off.
- Analytics is **off until you turn it on**. You are asked once when you first open the app, and you can change your answer here at any time.

**What is sent when it is on**
- Which screens are opened and which features are used — for example, that a questionnaire was completed.

**What is never sent**
- Names, photos, or anything else you typed.
- Your questionnaire answers or the results calculated from them.
- Any information about the people you added.

**Please note**
- Crash diagnostics work separately from this switch and stay active. A crash report describes the failure, the device model, and the operating system version. It contains none of your content.

---

## App Lock

**Menu path:** Settings → Security → **App Lock**

**What this does**
- Protects access to the app with a PIN code, so the content stays private if someone else picks up your device.
- **Change PIN** replaces the current code.
- Turning App Lock off removes the PIN from this device. You will need to set a new one if you turn it back on.

---

## Reset local data

**Menu path:** Settings → Data → **Reset local data**

**What this does**
- Removes locally stored app data on this device: your profile, the people you added, their photos, your answers, and calculated results.

**What this does not do**
- It does **not** delete your account.

---

## Delete account

**Menu path:** Settings → Account → **Delete account**

**What this does**
- Permanently deletes your SeeSi account and account access data stored on our servers.
- Removes locally stored app data on this device: your profile, the people you added, their photos, your answers, and calculated results.
- Removes the App Lock PIN from this device.
- Signs you out.

**Please note**
- If you last signed in a while ago, you will be asked to sign in once more before the account can be deleted. This confirms it is really you.
- If deletion does not go through, **nothing is removed** — your account and your data stay as they were.
- This action **cannot be undone**.

---

## Log out

**Menu path:** Settings → Account → **Log out**

Logging out signs you out of SeeSi on this device. It does not delete your account or your data.
